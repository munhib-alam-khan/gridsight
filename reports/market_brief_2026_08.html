August 2026 averaged Rs 32.2/kWh, down 11% on the month before and up 140% on August 2025.

217 of its 744 hours (29%) were at or above Rs 50, with a high of Rs 65.7 at 02:00 on 31 Aug.

The day split in two: midday hours (10:00-16:00) averaged Rs 10.2 while the night (00:00-06:00) averaged Rs 48.3. Solar output pushes cheap plants onto the margin at midday; after sunset the system falls back on expensive thermal plants.

For bulk consumers weighing open access

At August's prices, a 5 MW B-3 factory running around the clock would have paid Rs 48.5/kWh buying at the hourly market price through the wheeling auction, against Rs 30.6/kWh on its DISCO tariff: Rs 17.9/kWh more. Energy alone, grossed up for network losses, came to Rs 36.1/kWh.

The highest fixed contract price at which open access still breaks even for this factory: Rs 18.9/kWh at the generator through the auction, Rs 7.3/kWh outside it (where the Rs 12.94 stranded-cost charge applies).
